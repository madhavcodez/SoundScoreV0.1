SoundScore Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 19, 2026

SoundScore ("we", "us", or "our") is a music logging and rating application that helps you track, rate, and review the albums you listen to. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and what rights you have.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

When you create a SoundScore account, we collect:

  • Email address -- used for authentication and account recovery.
  • Handle (username) -- your public display name in the app.
  • Password -- stored only as a salted bcrypt hash; we never store or transmit your plaintext password.

1.2 User-Generated Content

As you use SoundScore, we store:

  • Album ratings (0--6 star scale), including per-track ratings.
  • Reviews -- text reviews you write for albums.
  • Lists -- curated album lists you create.
  • Listening history -- records of albums you have listened to, including timestamps.
  • Social activity -- follows, reactions, and comments on feed items.

1.3 Spotify Data

If you connect your Spotify account, we access the following data through the Spotify Web API with your explicit consent:

  • Recently played tracks -- to import your listening history into SoundScore.
  • Email address (from Spotify profile) -- for account linking purposes only.
  • Library data -- to identify albums you have saved.

We do not access your Spotify playlists, payment information, or any data beyond the scopes listed above. Spotify data is used solely to enrich your SoundScore listening history and is never sold to third parties.

1.4 Device Information

If you enable push notifications, we store your device token and platform type (iOS or Android). We also collect your IP address and user agent string for audit logging and abuse prevention purposes.

1.5 Analytics Events

We record anonymized analytics events (e.g., "recap generated", "recap viewed") to understand feature usage and improve the product. These events are tied to your user ID but contain no personally identifiable information beyond that.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Core functionality -- powering your ratings, reviews, lists, and listening history.
  • Social features -- displaying your activity to followers and showing your feed.
  • Weekly recaps -- generating personalized weekly listening summaries.
  • Push notifications -- sending notifications about social activity and recaps (configurable).
  • Abuse prevention -- rate limiting and audit logging to protect the platform.
  • Product improvement -- aggregated analytics to understand feature usage.

We do not sell your personal data to any third party.

3. Third-Party Services

SoundScore uses the following third-party services to operate:

  • Supabase (supabase.com) -- hosts our database and runs our API as Supabase Edge Functions. Your data is stored in Supabase-managed PostgreSQL. Supabase's privacy policy applies to infrastructure-level data handling.
  • Upstash (upstash.com) -- provides Redis caching for rate limiting and performance optimization. Cached data is ephemeral and automatically expires.
  • Spotify (spotify.com) -- provides music metadata and listening history import via their Web API. Spotify's Developer Terms of Service and Privacy Policy apply to data exchanged through their API. You can disconnect Spotify at any time from within the app.
  • MusicBrainz (musicbrainz.org) -- provides open-source music metadata for album search. No user data is sent to MusicBrainz.

4. Data Retention

  • Account data is retained for as long as your account is active.
  • Session tokens expire after 24 hours and expired sessions are automatically purged.
  • OAuth states are single-use and expire automatically.
  • Cached data (Redis) expires automatically within seconds to minutes depending on the data type.
  • Audit logs are retained for security and compliance purposes.

5. Your Rights

5.1 Data Export

You can export all of your data at any time using the Account Export feature in the app (Settings > Export My Data). This provides a complete JSON download of your profile, ratings, reviews, lists, listening history, social connections, and activity feed.

5.2 Account Deletion

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data using the Delete Account feature in the app (Settings > Delete Account). This action is irreversible and removes all your data from our systems, including:

  • Profile information
  • Ratings and reviews
  • Lists
  • Listening history
  • Social connections (follows)
  • Notification preferences and device tokens
  • Provider connections

5.3 Spotify Data Removal

You can disconnect your Spotify account at any time. When disconnecting, you may choose to also purge all listening data that was imported from Spotify.

5.4 Notification Control

You can configure which notification types you receive (social, recaps, comments, reactions) and set quiet hours through the notification preferences screen.

6. GDPR (European Economic Area)

If you are located in the EEA, you have the following rights under the GDPR:

  • Right of access -- use the Data Export feature.
  • Right to rectification -- edit your profile, ratings, and reviews in the app.
  • Right to erasure -- use the Delete Account feature.
  • Right to data portability -- use the Data Export feature (JSON format).
  • Right to object -- contact us to object to specific processing.
  • Right to restrict processing -- contact us to request processing restrictions.

Our legal basis for processing your data is: (a) performance of a contract (providing the SoundScore service), (b) legitimate interest (abuse prevention and product improvement), and (c) consent (Spotify data access, push notifications).

7. CCPA (California)

If you are a California resident, you have the following rights under the CCPA:

  • Right to know -- this policy describes what data we collect and how we use it.
  • Right to delete -- use the Delete Account feature or contact us.
  • Right to opt out of sale -- we do not sell your personal information.
  • Right to non-discrimination -- we will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

8. Security

We protect your data through the following measures:

  • Passwords are hashed using bcrypt with configurable salt rounds.
  • All database connections use SSL/TLS encryption.
  • API rate limiting prevents brute-force and abuse attacks.
  • Session tokens are generated using cryptographically secure random number generators.
  • OAuth flows use CSRF-protected state parameters.
  • Sensitive operations are audit-logged with scrubbed payloads (no passwords or tokens recorded).
  • HTML content is sanitized to prevent XSS.

9. Children's Privacy

SoundScore is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected data from a child under 13, please contact us so we can promptly delete it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Effective date" at the top of this page and notify you through the app if the changes are material.
